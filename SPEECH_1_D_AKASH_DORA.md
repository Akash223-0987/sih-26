---

## 🗣️ Exact Spoken Script (Word-for-Word Guide)

### 1. Welcome & Problem Statement Introduction (0:00 - 0:40)
> *"Respected judges and evaluation panel, good morning/afternoon.*  
> *I am **D Akash Dora**, presenting on behalf of our team for Problem Statement **26156** by the **National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO)**: **Universal Log Pre-processing Framework (ULPF)**.*
>
> *Today, modern enterprises, defense organizations, and critical national infrastructure operate in heterogeneous hybrid environments. Billions of log lines and telemetry events are generated every single day from firewalls, edge routers, servers, operating systems, cloud containers, databases, and microservices.*
>
> *However, these logs arrive in wildly fragmented formats: Syslog RFC 5424, legacy BSD RFC 3164, CEF from Palo Alto and Fortinet, LEEF from IBM QRadar, Windows Event CSVs, Apache web logs, and arbitrary JSON payloads."*

---

### 2. The Core Industry Pain Points (0:40 - 1:20)
> *"This diversity creates four massive bottlenecks for Security Operations Centers (SOCs):*
> 1. **Parser Fatigue & Inefficiency:** Security engineering teams spend thousands of hours building, testing, and maintaining vendor-specific regex parsers.
> 2. **Loss of Critical Forensic Evidence:** In traditional preprocessors, normalizing fields often strips away vendor-specific attributes, causing forensic blind spots during incident investigation.
> 3. **High Latency & Compute Overload:** Ingestion bottlenecks cause dropped events during traffic spikes, delaying threat correlation.
> 4. **Lack of AI-Ready Standardization:** Machine learning and threat correlation platforms cannot ingest raw, un-standardized strings effectively without heavy pre-processing overhead."*

---

### 3. The Need for a Universal Standard & Handoff (1:20 - 1:45)
> *"What NTRO and enterprise security teams need is an extensible, plug-and-play, and lossless framework that converts any device log into an analytics-ready representation at scale.*
>
> *To address this, our team has built **ULPF**.*
> *I will now hand over to **Aryan**, who will explain our core solution and design philosophy."*

---

- **The requirement for a standardized, high-speed, lossless framework**
